Our Process

From the first conversation
to the finished piece.

01
Inquiry & Consultation

Send us a message, a photo, a rough idea — anything. We take care of the rest. Design proposal always included, completely free.

02
Material selection

Yellow gold or white gold? Natural or lab diamond? We explain the difference — no pressure, no sales pitch. You decide.

03
Handcrafted production

Your piece is milled, set, engraved and polished — by hand. Inspected under a loupe before leaving the atelier. That takes 10 to 21 working days. No longer.

04
Lieferung & Unboxing

Your piece arrives in a gift box, with certificate and a handwritten note. Shipped insured. The unboxing is already an experience.
